The oneAZ design system names **Lexia** (Dalton Maag) for headings. Lexia is a licensed
AstraZeneca face and is **not** bundled: it is kept first in the heading stack as the donor
token string, and Roboto Slab renders in its place. Shipping Lexia requires a webfont licence.
